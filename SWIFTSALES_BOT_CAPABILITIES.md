# 🚀 SwiftSales Bot: Core Features & Capabilities

SwiftBot is a state-of-the-art AI pharmaceutical assistant designed to streamline healthcare distribution. Below is a comprehensive list of what SwiftBot can do for you.

---

## 🔍 1. Advanced Product Search & Information
SwiftBot has instant access to a database of **2,136+ pharmaceutical products**.
- **Comprehensive Details**: Get manufacturer info, pack sizes, and prices.
- **Clinical Guidance**: Ask about dosage, how to use a specific medicine, or side effects.
- **Smart Result Ranking**: Finds the most relevant medicine even with partial names.
- **Example**: *"What are the side effects of Augmentin?"* or *"How much does Panadol cost?"*

## 🛒 2. Intelligent Conversational Ordering
The bot guides you through the entire procurement process using natural language.
- **Voice-Like Ordering**: Just say what you want (e.g., *"Add 10 Pofen to my cart"*).
- **Cart Management**: Add, remove, update quantities, or clear your cart anytime.
- **Address Selection**: Remembers your delivery details for faster checkout.
- **Order Confirmation**: Structured summaries before final submission to simplify verification.

## 📦 3. Real-Time Stock Validation
Never worry about out-of-stock items at the last minute.
- **Live Inventory**: Checks stock status while you search and order.
- **Status Indicators**: Shows clear badges: ✅ Available, ⚠️ Low Stock, or ❌ Out of Stock.
- **Safety Checks**: Prevents ordering more than what is currently available.

## ⚕️ 4. Health Guidance & Safety
SwiftBot acts as a primary contact for common health queries, prioritizing safety.
- **OTC Recommendations**: Suggests appropriate over-the-counter products for symptoms like fever or pain.
- **Automatic Disclaimers**: Every response with health advice includes a professional reminder to consult a doctor.
- **Prescription Alerts**: Clearly marks medicines that require a registered prescription.

## 🚨 5. Emergency Safety Routing
SwiftBot is trained to recognize life-threatening situations instantly.
- **Crisis Detection**: Keywords like "overdose", "emergency", or "severe reaction" trigger an instant safety protocol.
- **Zero-Latency Routing**: Bypasses the AI brain for an immediate response directing users to the nearest hospital or emergency services.

## 🏢 6. Company & Operational Knowledge
Ask anything about Swift Sales Distributer's background and operations.
- **Business Operations**: Operating hours, delivery areas, and payment methods (COD, Bank Transfer, etc.).
- **Leadership & Vision**: Learn about the CEO, Malik Ejaz, and the company's 12-year journey since 2012.
- **Contact Info**: Instant access to WhatsApp, phone numbers, and location in Sardar Colony, RYK.

## 🧠 7. Specialized AI Capabilities
- **Context Continuity**: Remembers recent conversation history so you don't have to repeat yourself.
- **Procedural Help**: Ask "How do I order?" or "How do I pay?" for step-by-step interactive tutorials.
- **Fuzzy Intent Detection**: Understands you even if you make typos or use casual language.
- **100% Local Privacy**: Powered by **Ollama (llama3.2)**, meaning your queries never leave the secure local environment.

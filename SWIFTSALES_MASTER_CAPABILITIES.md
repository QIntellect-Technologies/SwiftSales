SwiftSales transformed its distribution network into a **24/7 Digital Clinic**. Below is the definitive list of everything our AI-powered bot can do, optimized for a professional **WhatsApp-First** experience.

---

### 📱 1. Accessibility & WhatsApp-First Experience
The biggest quality is **Zero-App Installation**. Users don't need a new website or app—they just save your number and start chatting.
- **🔡 Optimized Formatting**: Uses a *Strict Formatting Engine* for WhatsApp-ready bolding (asterisks) and clean bullet points for small screens.
- **⚡ Instant Feedback**: Programmed to "Mark as Read" instantly, giving users immediate comfort that their query is being processed.
- **📑 PDF Deep Links**: Generates links that open training guides to specific pages (e.g., `guide.pdf#page=45`) directly on the phone.
- **🎙️ Voice & Media Ready**: Built to handle "Snap & Send" images of X-rays or reports and process them immediately using internal Vision systems.

### 🧠 2. Intelligent Conversational Brain (Ollama)
Our bot doesn't just match keywords; it truly **understands** intent using its local LLM brain.
- **👥 3-Step Reply Logic**: Every message follows the professional **Acknowledge → Understand → Respond** framework to ensure empathy and accuracy.
- **👤 Permanent Memory**: Uses the phone number as a Unique Session ID. It remembers your symptoms and questions forever—even if you return a week later.
- **🌍 100% Local Privacy**: Powered by **Ollama (llama3.2)**, meaning no data leaves your secure local environment and there are zero API costs.

### 📦 3. Advanced Product Knowledge & Search
SwiftBot has instant access to **2,136+ pharmaceutical products** from 34+ manufacturers.
- **🔍 Hybrid Vector Search**: Finds the right medicine in under <100ms, even if the user makes typos or partial searches.
- **📊 Detailed Clinical Data**: Provides dosage, categories, pack sizes, uses for adults/children, and pricing instantly.
- **⚕️ Side Effect Analysis**: Can instantly list potential side effects and precautions for any medicine in the catalog.
